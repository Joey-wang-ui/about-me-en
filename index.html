PROJECT 01 / RESEARCHFinal year project · BUS303

Promotional language in beauty livestreams and short-term GMV response

An exploratory study of Florasis's official Douyin livestreams, examining the association between promotional language intensity and short-term GMV fluctuations, and connecting minute-level data to practical operating questions.

150Valid analysis samples
t + 3Minute-level event window
16.3–21.9%Range of model-predicted probabilities

EXPLORATORY RESULT The predicted probability rose from approximately 16.3% to 21.9%. This is an associational trend, not a causal finding.

01 / DATA

Collected livestream GMV, viewer counts, and related data from DouChaCha. Time-aligned and categorically coded promotional language against minute-level livestream data, yielding 150 valid samples.

02 / METHOD

Used an event-based observational design, treating each instance of promotional language as an event. Constructed a GMV-spike indicator for the t to t+3-minute window and used binary logistic regression to examine the association between language intensity and spike probability.

03 / INSIGHT

The exploratory model suggested a positive association. Combined the statistical results with the business context to recommend improvements in traffic timing, coupons, and product presentation during livestreams.

PROJECT 02 / RESEARCHPython / WRDS · IOM301

US financial data analysis and investment strategy research

Combined financial statements and stock-return data to examine the relationship between firm characteristics and returns. Developed data-informed strategy recommendations through cleaning, matching, and regression analysis.

29,266Firm-year observations
19Dataset variables
OLSRegression method

ACADEMIC RESEARCH Strategy recommendations demonstrate the research approach; they do not represent realized investment returns.

01 / DATA

Retrieved and merged Compustat financial data with CRSP monthly stock-return data through WRDS. Cleaned and matched the records to build a dataset of 29,266 firm-year observations and 19 variables.

02 / METHOD

Used Python for exploratory data analysis and OLS regression. Examined ROA, leverage, profit margin, firm size, and return volatility, and created correlation matrices, variable distributions, and annual-return trend visualizations.

03 / INSIGHT

Combined the statistical analysis and visual findings to recommend an investment approach that brings together fundamentals and price momentum.
